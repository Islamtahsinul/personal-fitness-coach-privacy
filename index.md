# Personal Fitness Coach — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 12 August 2026**

Personal Fitness Coach is a personal fitness application that connects to WHOOP to provide fitness and wellness insights.

## Information We Collect

When you connect your WHOOP account, the application may access data provided by WHOOP through its API, including health and fitness information such as:

- Sleep information
- Recovery information
- Workout information
- Heart-rate information
- Activity information
- Other data made available through the WHOOP API and authorised by the user

We only request access to information necessary for the functionality of the application.

## How We Use Your Information

Your WHOOP data is used to provide personalised fitness and wellness insights within the Personal Fitness Coach application.

We do not sell your personal information or WHOOP data.

We do not use your WHOOP data for advertising.

## Data Storage

WHOOP data may be temporarily processed or stored by the application to provide its functionality.

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

## Third-Party Services

Personal Fitness Coach uses the WHOOP API to access information that you authorise the application to access.

Your use of WHOOP is also subject to WHOOP's own terms and privacy policies.

## Disconnecting Your WHOOP Account

You may stop providing access to your WHOOP information by disconnecting or revoking the application's access through your WHOOP account.

## Data Deletion

If you would like your data associated with Personal Fitness Coach to be deleted, please contact us using the contact information provided below.

## Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or requests concerning your personal information, please contact the maintainer by opening an issue at https://github.com/Islamtahsinul/personal-fitness-coach-privacy/issues (please do not include personal or health data in a public issue; ask for a private contact route instead).

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
